01The Place

Vilnica · Julian Alps

Some places are visited. Some places are remembered.

iLocation

Where the road gives up

At 1,247 metres, above the last village and below the first snowline, Vilina sits where the asphalt ends and the forest begins.

iiArchitecture

Built low, on purpose

Eighteen rooms in larch, limestone and slate. Set into the slope so the mountain stays the tallest thing in every window.

iiiNature

Fog at four, gone by ten

Spruce forest to the north, a glacial lake to the east, and a cloud that arrives in the afternoon and leaves when it is ready.

ivPhilosophy

Few guests, long silences

In Slavic folklore a vila watches over forest and water. We keep her rules: very few visitors, nothing hurried, nothing to prove.
